What happened

Two bodies of work for Mimi's home chocolate business. First, a print collateral kit in a new mimis-print workspace: a two-sided business card with a QR code to the website, a 5×7 counter sign explaining base-price-plus-quote pricing, and a US-Letter invoice that is a genuinely fillable PDF form. Second, a /reviews page for the website, built on its own branch as an idea for later — nothing touched the live site. Every piece reuses the brand tokens straight from the site source (cocoa, cream, blush, gold; Parisienne, Fraunces, Nunito) so print and web match exactly.

Shipped, by area

Business card (3.5×2 in + bleed, front/back)

  • Cream front: logo, script wordmark, "Made with Love"
  • Cocoa back: QR code to mimischocolatebites.com in a cream tile, @mimischocolatebites handle, site URL
  • QR generated as vector SVG in brand cocoa, so it stays crisp at any print size

Pricing sign (5×7 in, fits a standard frame)

  • Gold double frame, script "A Note on Pricing" headline
  • Copy rewritten in original wording: listed prices are starting points; final quote follows theme, colors, design details, personal touches, and add-ons
  • IG handle + site URL footer

Fillable invoice (US Letter)

  • 46 real AcroForm fields: invoice no./date, bill-to, occasion and theme, 8 line-item rows, notes, payment, subtotal through total due
  • Works in macOS Preview, Acrobat, and phone PDF viewers; prints clean for handwriting too
  • Field coordinates in make-invoice.mjs mirror the inch-positioned zones in invoice.html, so design and form stay in lockstep

Website: /reviews page (branch only)

  • "Kind words from sweet people" in the Rich & Moody style: raining cake pops, cream review cards, five blush hearts instead of stars
  • Card grid collapses to one column on phones; same Back-link treatment as /contact
  • "Enjoyed your bites?" CTA: gold Leave a review button plus a DM us instead Instagram button
  • All six reviews are sample copy, labeled in the code; REVIEW_URL points at /contact until Mimi has a Google review link
  • Not linked from the landing yet — a design call for when it ships
