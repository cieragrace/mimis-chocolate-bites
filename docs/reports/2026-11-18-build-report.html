What happened

Two asks from Mimi: put the Colorado cottage-food disclosure on the site, and add a gallery of her Instagram photos and videos. Her public reels were pulled logged-out, muxed from Instagram's split video and audio streams, re-encoded to 720p, and given clean end-frame posters because Instagram's own covers carry a baked-in play icon. Feedback rounds made the grid cleaner and put photos ahead of videos. Three redesign directions were prototyped in parallel worktrees, and Mimi's pick became a 3-second auto-rotating carousel. The fine print turned into a scrolling pink ribbon footer. Last came her 41 downloaded photos: 38 kept, cropped to 4:5, EXIF and GPS stripped since the repo is public. Each stage went to a preview link first, then shipped and was checked live on 11 device sizes.

Gallery (/gallery)

  • Folder-driven: drop a file in src/assets/gallery/; gallery.json overrides alt, caption, order, hidden
  • "Recent treats" photo grid first: 2/3/4 columns of 4:5 tiles, 12 shown plus "See all 38 photos"; all photos render without JS
  • "Watch it come together" video carousel: scroll-snap, seamless clone loop, 3s auto-advance
  • Rotation pauses on hover, focus, open viewer, hidden tab and off-screen, resumes after 6s idle, has a pause button
  • Lightbox is one <dialog> per section
  • 11 reels at 720p H.264 with +faststart, end-frame posters with black bars cropped
  • Fixed: reduced-motion CSS hid every slide, so those visitors saw an empty gallery

Cottage disclosure

  • Wording single-sourced in src/data/cottage.ts, verbatim
  • Pink ribbon marquee footer on landing, /pricing and /gallery: Fraunces italic, heart separators, 70s loop
  • Pauses on hover/focus; screen readers get the sentence once; static and wrapped under reduced motion
  • Static notice stays under Submit on the order form

Landing + SEO

  • Gallery button added to the landing
  • Fixed: 2px overflow on 320px phones (Galaxy S9+); landing fits one screen again
  • /pricing and /gallery added to sitemap.xml

Verification
