What happened

Mimi supplied a full order-form field spec, a revised six-tile By the Dozen flyer, and a printed fall production sheet. The contact form was rebuilt from a 4-field note into a structured order form, iterated live across nine deploys as the treat-group layout settled (chocolate preference moved into the treat group, back out, then back in once premade flavors gave the grouping its final shape). Every deploy was built locally, screenshot-checked in a real browser, shipped to Cloudflare Pages, and curl-verified against the production domain. A mock order was submitted end to end to confirm what lands in Mimi's inbox, and the Colorado cottage-food label problem got a researched fix with a visual 2″ sticker mock-up.

Order form (/contact)

  • Rebuilt to Mimi's 12-field spec; only name and email required
  • Repeatable treat groups: type dropdown, quantity, premade flavor, "or build your own" chocolate + toppings; "+ Add another treat" clones the whole group (max 6, × to remove)
  • Premade flavor menu from her fall sheet: 10 flavors + Gluten Free + Build my own; Biscoff Cookie Butter swapped in for Caramel Apple
  • Colorado cottage-food disclosure, her wording verbatim, in fine print under Submit
  • Empty optional fields stripped before send so order emails stay clean
  • Treat fields renumbered per group ("Treat 1", "Treat 1 quantity", …) in the email
  • Card widened to max-w-2xl; placeholders no longer clip
  • Fixed: browser autofill prepending "+1" to phone numbers (autocomplete="tel-national")

Pricing page (/pricing)

  • New six-tile By the Dozen flyer live: adds Strawberries ($18/$32) and Marshmallows ($16/$28)
  • Same crop treatment as before: www strip removed, Denver CO ribbon kept (512×620 of 640); original archived in flyers/
  • Alt-text pricing rewritten for all six tiles (screen readers + SEO)
  • Side-by-side flyers now render the same height: grid columns proportional to each flyer's aspect ratio

Print (mimis-print)

  • 2″ sticker system preview: pretty front sticker + fine-print back label (round and 2×3″ options) carrying every required label element
  • Researched CO Cottage Foods Act labeling: "legible print" only, no minimum font size; point-of-sale sign uses a shorter statement than the package label
