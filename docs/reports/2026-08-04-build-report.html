What happened

The pricing page got its second iteration and its public debut. Iteration 1 had rebuilt Mimi's two flyers as HTML; the call this session was to show her actual artwork instead. The page now embeds both flyers as images inside the site's moody frame, cropped only to remove the printed www.mimischocolatebites.com strips, which are replaced by real links to Instagram and the order form. After side-by-side desktop layout and equal-width phone stacking were verified in both viewports, the branch was merged to main and deployed to mimischocolatebites.com, with a new See pricing button on the landing page so visitors can actually find it. Every change was screenshot-verified locally before deploying, and the live domain was curl-checked after.
